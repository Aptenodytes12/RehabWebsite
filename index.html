Why Local, Specialised Care Matters

Addiction in Dehradun, by the Numbers

A peer-reviewed study of clients at de-addiction centres across Dehradun district — approved by the Institutional Ethics Committee of Government Doon Medical College — found alcohol to be the most common substance of abuse (61.7%), followed by tobacco (15.6%), with most patients beginning use after the age of 18. Understanding these local patterns is part of why treatment here needs to be built around Dehradun's specific realities, not a generic national template.

Source: Zahin et al., "Epidemiological Correlates of Substance Abuse Among In-Facility Clients of De-addiction and Rehabilitation Centres of District Dehradun," Cureus, 2023 →
