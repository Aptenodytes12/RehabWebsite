The First Step in Recovery

Why Medically Supervised Detox Is Essential

Withdrawal from alcohol or drugs can bring on symptoms ranging from mild discomfort to serious medical complications, depending on the substance and duration of use. Our detox unit in Dehradun provides continuous monitoring by doctors and nursing staff, so any complications are caught and managed immediately.

Once the body has safely cleared the substance, patients move into our structured therapy and counselling programs to address the psychological side of recovery.
